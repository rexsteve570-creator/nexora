About market data
Chart data is provided by TradingView. Coin prices displayed in the list are read from NEXORA's market-price database. These data sources may update at different times. A missing or outdated database price will be shown as unavailable rather than replaced with an invented value. Market prices are informational and do not represent a completed trade or a guaranteed execution price.
