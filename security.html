Enable two-factor authentication

You will need an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or another compatible TOTP app.

  1. Open your authenticator app on your phone.
  2. Choose Add account or tap the plus button.
  3. Scan the QR code below, or enter the setup key manually.
  4. Enter the six-digit code displayed in the app to verify setup.

Two-factor authentication is enabled

Your account has a verified authenticator factor. Your sign-in page must also enforce the additional verification challenge before allowing access to protected pages.

Keep your authenticator app available when signing in. Removing the factor can reduce your account's protection.

Disable two-factor authentication?

This removes the selected verified authenticator from your account. You will need to confirm your current six-digit code before it can be removed.
