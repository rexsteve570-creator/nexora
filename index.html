FAQ

Common questions

What is the minimum deposit?

The minimum deposit is $1, or its equivalent in the coin you choose, so you can start small.

Which cryptocurrencies are supported?

Aitivest supports 24 assets, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Tether, USD Coin, BNB, Solana, XRP, Toncoin, Dogecoin, Cardano and more. The full list is shown above.

Do I need to verify my identity?

Yes. Identity verification helps protect your account and meet applicable requirements. You upload an ID document and a selfie, and your submission is reviewed before approval.

How do withdrawals work?

Pick the coin and network, enter your wallet address and amount, review the fee, then confirm with your two-factor code. Always double-check the address and network, because blockchain transfers can't be reversed.

What is the profit countdown?

It's a timer that shows when your next profit calculation is due. The countdown itself doesn't credit earnings; credited amounts appear in your account records.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. Digital assets are volatile and all investing carries risk, including the loss of capital. Only invest what you can afford to lose.
