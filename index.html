Frequently asked questions.

What is AITIVEST?

AITIVEST is a digital asset management platform designed to provide clients with an online environment for managing supported digital assets.

What is the minimum deposit?

The planned minimum deposit is $1 USDT, subject to the applicable asset and network requirements.

Which digital assets are supported?

Supported market listings include BTC, ETH, USDT, BNB, SOL, XRP, TON, DOGE, ADA, TRX, LTC and AVAX, subject to availability.

Can I withdraw my assets?

Available withdrawals depend on supported assets, networks, account status, applicable checks and any disclosed network or processing fees.

Does AITIVEST guarantee investment returns?

No investment outcome should be treated as guaranteed. Digital assets involve significant risks, including volatility and potential loss. Review the Risk Disclosure before using the platform.
